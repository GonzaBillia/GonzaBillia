## About me

I'm a **Backend Developer / Software Engineer** with experience designing, building, and maintaining enterprise microservice ecosystems focused on **internal operations, customer management, logistics, HR, loyalty programs, authentication, notifications, and observability**.

I enjoy working on systems that connect different business domains through clean APIs, modular architecture, async processing, centralized authentication, and reliable integrations.

My background also includes **QA Automation**, which helps me approach software development with a strong focus on quality, maintainability, traceability, and production reliability.

> Most of my production work was developed in private company repositories. This profile focuses on the technologies, architecture patterns, and personal/demo projects that reflect my experience.

## What I do

- Design and develop backend microservices using **NestJS**, **TypeScript**, and modular architecture.
- Build internal systems and integrations using **Python**, **FastAPI**, **React**, **Next.js**, and relational databases.
- Implement authentication and authorization flows with **Keycloak**, API Gateways, token exchange, and reusable AuthZ packages.

## Enterprise experience highlights

### Microservices ecosystem
Designed and built the technical foundation of a segmented and reusable service ecosystem that enabled multiple internal systems to communicate through REST APIs, centralized authentication, shared identity, notifications, logistics, customer data, and operational traceability.

### Authentication & authorization
Implemented a self-hosted **Keycloak** service for centralized user management, roles, permissions, application clients, credentials, and service-to-service communication through token exchange.

Built an **API Gateway with NestJS** to centralize login, proxy access, and authentication flows across microservices.

Developed a reusable **AuthZ package** for Express and NestJS services to simplify integration with the gateway and authorization model.

### Notifications platform
Designed and developed a **notification microservice** with NestJS, Redis, and BullMQ to connect internal services with external providers such as email and WhatsApp platforms.

### Business systems
Worked on backend services for:

- HR management, scheduling, branches, collaborators, attendance, and performance traceability.
- Chronic patient tracking, recurrence calculation, semi-automated medication orders, and logistics integration.
- Customer identity and information management across multiple services.
- Loyalty points with earning/spending rules and billing integration.
- Customer-facing web app integrating authentication, points, customer data, recurring orders, and purchase history.
